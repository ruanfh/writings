# The sword
Immortality was granted to he who held the sword.  
But no man was worthy of it, until the arrival of the king.  
Who earned the sword by sheer might and benevolence.  

"Is it fair for the son to die while the father lives on?"  
Asked the warlock, filling the prince's heart with self-righteousness.  
The prince stole the longsword and split it into two short swords.  

One half for his father and the other for himself.  
But the breakage of the sword fulfilled the warlock's spell.  
Which redirected immortality to the warlock himself.  

Now the warlock rules as a tyrant, to the despair of the common folk.  
One day, the swords will be reforged into one, dispelling the curse.  
That day, the worthy heir from the frail lineage shall rise immortal.
