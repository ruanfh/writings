# The sword of old
He who held the sword was granted immortality.
The mightiest among mortals was he, the king.
Who sacrificed everything to earn the sword.

"Is it fair for the son to die while the father lives on?"
Asked the warlock, filling the young prince's heart with self-righteousness.
The prince stole the longsword and split it into two short swords.

One half for his father and the other for himself.
But the breakage of the sword fulfilled the warlock's spell.
Which redirected immortality to the warlock himself.

Now the warlock rules as a tyrant, to the despair of the common folk.
One day, the swords will be reforged into one, thus the curse will be dispelled.
Time wiped the old lineage, but a new king will earn, once again, the sword of old.
