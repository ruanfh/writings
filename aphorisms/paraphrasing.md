# Paraphrasing aphorisms

Right makes might, strength makes unity.  

> Paraphrase of:  
> - “Unity makes strength.” (proverb)  
> - “Might is Right.” (Ragnar Redbeard)

**Explanation:**  
Acting rightfully makes people stronger — more solidary, productive, and giving. That strength builds true unity: community, family, and the like.
