# Genesis cosmogony reading

This reading makes no claim to authority.

It is limited strictly to Genesis 1:1–2:3.
From this point forward, “text” refers only to that passage.

## Summary of the reading

| Symbol | Day | Entity     | Step                       | Pythagoras |
| ------ | --- | ---------- | -------------------------- | ---------- |
| 1      | 0   | God        | Before the start           | Monad      |
| 2      | 1   | Archetypes | Light and shadow           | Dyad       |
| 4      | 3   | Universe   | Sky until land and sea     | Tetrad     |
| 8      | 7   | Sentience  | Sun and moon until resting | Octad      |

## Before the text
Before the text even begins, there is already God.
Let one be the symbol of God.
Let each day's action be plus one.

A system with only one element has no inner borders.
Therefore such a system is truly whole and indivisible.
A system with only one element has no outer borders.
Therefore such a system is truly singular and unique.

Note that there is no one-element system in nature.
Even the partially individualized soul is not singular.
The cosmos as a whole is full of internal divisions.

## Day 1

| God's presence | God's action at Day 1 | the creation at Day 1 |
| -------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| 1              | +1                    | =2                    |

> God creates light and see that it is good.

This is the first and biggest tension in the whole text.
Light symbolizes the good and darkness or shadows the evil.
Note that evil is not a presence but a mere absence.

The good is presence and truth, the evil is absence and falsehood:

| Field   | Light   | Shadow |
| ------- | ------- | ------ |
| Moral   | Good    | Evil   |
| Ontic   | Present | Absent |
| Boolean | True    | False  |

Let two be the symbol of the Platonic Forms or the Archetypes.
Two has one circular permutation, meaning the Archetypes are immutable (read-only).

Everything can be reduced to a binary representation, given enough storage.
The Archetypes (two) lack actuality, at this stage things are only represented, not embodied.

## Day 2

| the creation at Day 1 | God's action at Day 2 | the creation at Day 2 |
| --------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| 2                     | +1                    | =3                    |

> God creates the sky or firmament.

Before that, everything was underwater.

Now there is water above the sky and underneath it.
Which means we have three elements being highlighted:
- Waters above
- Water below
- The sky

Three is the first number that produces a closed polygon.
Meaning the system with three elements has a face but no volume.

Let three be Time.
Note that Time has three elements:
- Past
- Present
- Future

Three has two circular permutations, meaning Time has two directions, back and forward.

## Day 3

| the creation at Day 2 | God's action at Day 3 | the creation at Day 3 |
| --------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| 3                     | +1                    | =4                    |

> God separates dry land and sea.

Four elements are highlighted:

|      | Wet          | Dry      |
| ---- | ------------ | -------- |
| High | Waters above | Sky      |
| Low  | Sea          | Dry land |

Let four be the whole cosmos.
The growth of plants indicates that the cosmos already contains life but not sentience yet.

Four points are the minimum to build a solid.
Four has volume, therefore actuality.

The circular permutation of Four is six, space has six directions:
- Upward
- Downward
- Forward
- Backward
- Rightward
- Leftward

## Day 4

| the creation at Day 3 | God's action at Day 4 | the creation at Day 4 |
| --------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| 4                     | +1                    | =5                    |

> God creates the sun, the moon, and the stars.

All inside the sky, which show how big the sky is and how far the waters above are.

There are five layers above the ground:
- Atmosphere
- Moon
- Sun
- Stars
- Waters above

Five is four plus one. In other words the unity of One in the mutability of the Cosmos.
One that remains inside Four. That perfectly aligns with the planets and stars that are a unity traveling across the cosmos.

Let five be the symbol of bodies. Note that vegetation although being homeostatic bodies are not perceived as singular bodies but rather vegetation, i.e. a tapestry or canopy.

The enlightened bodies are analogous to sentient beings, bodies with their own light.
The celestial bodies draw attention to the light and dark contrast.

The five layers are hierarchical, which highlights the hierarchical nature of attention, the light of sentience.

In a way this day can be thought of as the day of the preconscious.

## Day 5

| the creation at Day 4 | God's action at Day 5 | the creation at Day 5 |
| --------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| 5                     | +1                    | =6                    |

> God creates the birds and the fishes.

Both move in three dimensions.
One underwater other over the sky.
Three dimensions underwater and three dimensions over the sky, six dimensions in total.

Six is two times three. That means it has all the properties of two (Archetypes) and three (Time).

Sentience has an immutable core, just like an archetype and has a sense of time. Note that this combination produces the append-only or add-only behavior of consciousness, where information can only be gathered and is never lost (unless something disrupts the normal behavior).
In other words, each new experienced day becomes part of the inner core of sentience, thus add-only.

Day five is when sentience is first explicitly mentioned, the birds and fishes draw attention to the dry and wet.

## Day 6

| the creation at Day 5 | God's action at Day 6 | the creation at Day 6 |
| --------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| 6                     | +1                    | =7                    |

> God creates the land creatures including man and woman.

Note that the land creatures are more limited in movement than the birds and fishes.
This step of sentience building is a constraint rather than an expansion.

Seven is prime, it can't be reduced to a combination of prior numbers, so six plus one equals seven.

Let seven be the number of reason.
Humans (rational beings) are put in charge of the other animals (unbounded sentience).

Land creatures draw attention to the ups and downs of the surface of the earth that constrain their movement.

## Day 7

| the creation at Day 6 | God's action at Day 7 | the creation at Day 7 |
| --------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------- |
| 7                     | +1                    | =8                    |

> God rests.

Sentience is finally completed, and the rest is part of its creation.

God's rest means the autonomy of sentience.
Human sentience is only finished when God can rest.

Eight can be reduced to two to the power of three.
Or two times four, i.e. the two distinctions from Day one: light and shadow, times the four types from Day three.

Light is good, darkness is the absence of good: evil.
Dry can be thought of as principled and wet as adaptable or pragmatic.
Finally high and low can be thought of as outward and inward.
That produces eight types:

| Clarity  | Dryness    | Height  | Label   |
| -------- | ---------- | ------- | ------- |
| learned  | principled | outward | Knight  |
| learned  | principled | inward  | Monk    |
| learned  | pragmatic  | outward | Ranger  |
| learned  | pragmatic  | inward  | Scholar |
| ignorant | principled | outward | Reaper  |
| ignorant | principled | inward  | Wretch  |
| ignorant | pragmatic  | outward | Tyrant  |
| ignorant | pragmatic  | inward  | Rogue   |

Let the Cosmos be four. Sentience being eight contains the cosmic structure within plus another set of four over it.
In other words the Cosmos has Time the Soul has Time-in-the-Soul, the Cosmos has space, the Soul has Space-in-the-Soul.
